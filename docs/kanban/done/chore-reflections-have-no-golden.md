# chore-reflections-have-no-golden — Reflections have no golden

The golden suite has eleven cases and not one of them is about reflections. This card adds a
reflection scene to `engine/assets/` and a case that pins it, so the pass that is 12% of the
frame stops being the one nothing checks.

The cases are `lit`, `albedo`, `normal`, `depth`, `ssao`, `msaa1`, `no-rt`, `emissive`, the
cascade scene, `particles`, `skin` and `physics`
([the `CASES` array](../../../scripts/golden.sh)). `SSR` is **0.643 ms of the
demo's 5.377 ms frame** and it has both a screen-space march and a ray-traced path — 0.271 ms
with `--no-rt`, so the two paths differ by more than a factor of two in cost and are not the
same algorithm. `no-rt` exercises the march incidentally, as one of many differences in that
arm; nothing isolates either.

~~**A reflection scene used to exist and is gone.**
[the ambient-asymmetry note in `shadeSample`](../../../engine/shaders/lighting_body.glsl) cites a measurement
taken on `reflect.gltf` — "world 0.955, mirror 1.000" — and `engine/assets/` now holds only
`emissive.gltf`, `particles.gltf`, `physics.gltf` and `skin.gltf`. The scene the ambient
asymmetry argument was settled on cannot be re-rendered, which means that argument cannot be
re-checked.~~

**False.** `reflect.gltf` is at `game/demo/assets/reflect.gltf`, 627 KB, still generated by
`scripts/make_composite_scene.py` and still listed in `README.md` and `tooling.md`. The card
looked only in `engine/assets/`. It is re-renderable today, and the new case therefore takes a
different name — `res:/` searches the game tree first, so a second `reflect.gltf` under
`engine/assets/` would silently shadow the demo's.

This card is a **prerequisite for
[chore-screen-space-reflections-cost-full-resolution-for-a-low-frequency-signal](chore-screen-space-reflections-cost-full-resolution-for-a-low-frequency-signal.md)**,
and that is the reason to do it now rather than eventually. A half-resolution SSR change would
otherwise land with no automated coverage of the thing it changes.

`inspection` is on the verification line honestly: the gap *is* the finding, and until the case
exists there is nothing to run.

The scene wants a mirror-flat surface and a rough one in the same frame — the roughness cutoff
defaults to 0.4 ([the `render.ssrRoughnessCutoff` row](../../../engine/core/Settings.h)), so a case that only
shows one side of that threshold pins half the pass — plus something with a shadow in the
reflection, which is what
[`rayshadow.glsl`](../../../engine/shaders/rayshadow.glsl)'s "both paths, one calculation"
argument is about.

**Note the suite count changes.** Cards written after this one name `golden-12`, and the token
already exists in `scripts/kanban.py`'s vocabulary from when the suite last had twelve.

**Provenance.** Every figure above was measured at `37c2d44`, before the per-view refactor (C31, C32) landed. Re-run the arm before acting on a delta.

## Verification

- `scripts/golden.sh snap` then `scripts/golden.sh check` — the new case reproducible
  byte-identical across two runs, which is the actual claim a baseline makes.
- The existing eleven unchanged: adding a case must not disturb them.
- Run it once with `--no-rt` as well, and decide on the card whether the march deserves its own
  case or whether one scene under two flags is enough.

## Outcome

**Two cases, not one, and the suite is thirteen.** `mirror.gltf` is generated by
`scripts/make_test_scene.py` — the same one-line mechanism as `emissive`, `particles`, `skin`
and `physics`, so it is tracked as a generator rather than as an asset — and `scripts/golden.sh`
gained `mirror` and `mirror-no-rt`. Case count 11 → 13, byte-identical on three separate check
runs (`0/1440000 pixels over tolerance 2, mean delta 0.0000`), the eleven existing baselines
untouched and unchanged. A fourth run after the `rayshadow.glsl` comment correction returned
`all 13 cases match`; the run before it hit `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST` at `vkCreateDevice` on
`albedo`, which is this machine and not the change — the new HARNESS classification named it
correctly and stopped the suite, and the re-run was clean.

The scene: a 12x16 mirror floor at **roughness 0.02**, four metallic spheres at **0.05, 0.25,
0.35, 0.65**, a dielectric wall at **0.90** and a pylon at **0.75**, under one directional
light. `ssrRoughnessCutoff` is 0.4 with the fade starting at 0.2, so 0.02/0.05/0.25 run at or
near full strength, 0.35 sits in the tail of the fade at roughFade ~0.16, and 0.65/0.75/0.90
take the early-out — the branch most of a real frame's surface area takes, and the one a case on
one side of the threshold cannot reach.

**The shadow had to go on the wall, and that is the thing the card did not anticipate.** A
metallic floor has no diffuse term for a shadow to darken, so a shadow cast onto the mirror is
invisible and a scene arranged the obvious way would claim coverage it does not have. The pylon
shadows the rough wall, the floor reflects the wall, and the shadow appears once directly and
once in the reflection — which is the `rayshadow.glsl` "both paths, one calculation" claim, in
one frame. Reflection ray length there is ~4.5 units, inside `ssrMaxDistance` 8. One other
composition fix during bring-up: the wall at albedo 0.66 under 6 lux tonemapped to clipped
white, and a clipped backdrop is one a diff cannot see through; it is 0.42 under 2.5 lux now.

**The `--no-rt` question, answered: two cases.** The arms are not the same picture. With ray
query the floor reflects the wall seamlessly below the horizon and the smooth spheres reflect
sky; under `--no-rt` a ray that leaves the screen finds nothing, so the same floor is black
across most of its area, the spheres are dark, and ragged stair-step fringes appear where the
march does find the sphere-floor contacts. That fringe is precisely what a half-resolution SSR
change would move. The decisive argument is that the existing `no-rt` case renders **Sponza**,
which by this card's own premise has no smooth surface anywhere — pinning only the traced arm
would have left the march exactly as uncovered as before, which is half of the gap this card
exists to close.

**Three things in the tree turned out to be false**, one of them on this card and struck through
above. `scripts/kanban.py` accepted only `golden-11` and `golden-12`, and its own comment named
a third spelling as the point to take the count out of the token; that is now done — `golden` is
the token, `golden-<n>` stays accepted so cards in `done/` keep saying which suite they ran
against. And `rayshadow.glsl`'s header claimed the cascade maps *and* the punctual atlas "were
removed whole rather than kept as a portable fallback", and that a device without ray query
"gets the unshadowed variant": `--no-rt` demonstrably renders the pylon's shadow,
`engine/shaders/shadow.{vert,frag,glsl}` exist, `recordShadows` and `recordPunctualShadows` run,
and `featureKey()` still feeds `shadowsEnabled` to the non-traced variants. Only the cascades
went. The header is corrected.

**What this case cannot do, stated so nobody re-derives it.** It does not re-check the
ambient-asymmetry measurement, and the reason is not the scene. The asymmetry is
`constantAmbient(diffuseColor, occlusion * ssao)` in `lighting_body.glsl` against
`constantAmbient(diffuseColor, bakedAO)` in `raytrace.glsl`; `frame.ambient` comes from
`Renderer::ambientColor`, which defaults to zero and is set only from `GameSetup::ambientColor`,
with no CLI flag to raise it. A golden case runs the engine with no game, so the term the
asymmetry lives in is multiplied by zero. The geometry is right for it — `--debug-view ssao`
shows real darkening at the wall-floor crease and the sphere contacts, no material carries an
occlusion texture so `bakedAO` is exactly 1.0, and world crease and reflected crease are both in
frame — but re-measuring needs a game that sets an ambient, which is how the original number was
taken.
